# CommonTab

**Know what you owe, what you're owed, and what to do next.**

CommonTab is a shared payment workspace for clubs, group trips and community groups. Every shared cost goes in one record with its purpose, amount, participants, receipts, deadline and proof of payment. That replaces scattered group chats and spreadsheets.

## How it works

1. **Create a group** and invite members.
2. **Post a payment request** with what it's for, how much, how it's split and when it's due. Attach the receipt.
3. **Members confirm** their share.
4. **Pay through the service you already use** (Venmo, Zelle, etc.) and attach proof.
5. **Everyone sees the status:** who's paid, who hasn't and what's overdue.

AI helps draft requests from a receipt or a quick message and flags anything missing. People always confirm before anything is final.

## What CommonTab isn't

CommonTab doesn't hold money, store balances or process payments. It coordinates around the payment services people already use.

## Status

Early development. The first release focuses on student clubs and group trips in USD.
